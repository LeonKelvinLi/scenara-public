Scenara translation credits

Market translations use the OPUS-MT English-Portuguese and English-Chinese
models, packaged by Argos Open Technologies (model package version 1.9).

Authors: Jörg Tiedemann and Santhosh Thottingal.
OPUS-MT — Building open translation services for the World.
Proceedings of the 22nd Annual Conference of the European Association for
Machine Translation (EAMT), Lisbon, 2020.

Original model weights: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International.
License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Model source: https://github.com/argosopentech/argospm-index
Runtime: https://github.com/OpenNMT/CTranslate2

Scenara runs these models locally using int8 inference and paragraph chunks.
Cached translations may include editorial corrections. Original resolution
rules remain available through the original-text control and source link.
